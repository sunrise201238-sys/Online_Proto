The print at 2× — A's still corner
The print — the same in A, B and C: 112 × 58, a white matte with the ink rim, the gun's render (the game's own weapons/*.png) on a pale wash of the class colour (lavender for a rifle; cyan AR, lime SMG, yellow SG, magenta MG, ink SR), set 7° off and tucked under the clip at the card's top-right, over the folder's edge. The free corner — bottom-left, away from the clip — is curled: cut off the print, the folded back showing, shaded. On the phone (zoom ≈ 0.7) it is about 78 × 41 CSS px.

A stands still. B breathes: the curl grows from 18 to 30 px and settles, once every 3 s. C flips the whole print over on a tap (the board does it on its own every 4 s) — the back is ruled like the memo and names the gun.

Say A, B or C (and any change — size, where the curl is, the wash), and it goes onto PT and the 0.9.5 line.
